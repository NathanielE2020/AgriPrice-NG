Evaluation methodology

Overall MAPE and R² are medians of genuine per-series or per-pool validation scores; the observation count is the actual validation coverage represented by the selected summary scope. RMSE, MAE and bias are omitted from overall comparisons because raw errors across different commodities, units and price scales are not directly comparable.

Exact-series chronological validation and compatible-pool group-held-out validation are separate evaluation scopes, not a controlled head-to-head comparison. Evaluation dates and coverage may differ by series. Research test metrics remain audit-only and are not mixed into validation results or used for model selection.

Evaluation dates appear with saved results

ABOUT THE PROJECT

Making food-price information easier to explore.

AgriPrice-NG is a final-year Computer Science project that helps users explore Nigerian agricultural prices and estimated future movements.

AG
OUR PURPOSE

Understand how agricultural prices move across Nigeria.

Farmers, traders, households and researchers benefit from clear access to food-price information. AgriPrice-NG organizes available WFP market observations and NBS monthly summaries so users can explore recorded prices over time and consider estimates for future months.

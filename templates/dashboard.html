Model and methodology details

The forecast target is shown here.

  • Multi-month forecasts are recursive: each predicted month is used when constructing later forecast steps. If the latest actual is before the current month, internally generated bridge estimates are used to reach the future window; they are not actual observations and are not returned as history.
  • The selected model has the lowest RMSE on the same chronological validation period, including the naive last-observation baseline. Test observations are not used for selection.
  • Validation is a chronological rolling-origin one-step assessment: each target uses only observations available before its month. These validation metrics do not measure a recursive six-month forecast directly.
  • Reported RMSE, MAE, MAPE, R² and validation observation count describe the validation period.
  • Missing months are not interpolated or forward-filled. Training examples use the prior three actual observations and record the elapsed calendar gaps; the next target is an actually observed month.
  • WFP actual market series and NBS item/geography reports are forecast separately and are never combined.
  • Forecasts are estimates, not guaranteed prices. No confidence intervals are provided.

DATA AND METHODOLOGY

Two sources, separate evidence.

AgriPrice-NG analyzes Nigerian agricultural price data without assuming that different source definitions are interchangeable.

AG
FINAL-YEAR COMPUTER SCIENCE PROJECT

Machine Learning Based Analysis and Prediction of Agricultural Commodities Prices in Nigerian Market.

WFP contains dated commodity, market, state, unit, price type, currency and price-flag records. NBS provides selected-food national and geopolitical-zone monthly averages. NBS source item labels are preserved because standardized units are not supplied in the workbook.

CURRENT STATUS

What is available now

  1. 01
    Sources verified

    WFP CSVs and NBS monthly workbooks/PDF reports are readable. Original downloaded files remain unchanged.

  2. 02
    Keep sources distinct

    WFP market observations and NBS national/zone summaries are separate; price flags and exact item definitions are retained.

  3. 03
    Gap-aware forecasting

    Missing target prices are not assigned or filled. Historical training features use actual prior observations and explicitly record calendar gaps.

  4. 04
    Source freshness limits interpretation

    WFP pure-actual history currently ends in April 2026 and NBS reporting in May 2026. Future-dated estimates can be requested, but none uses sufficiently recent data to represent current market conditions.

Coverage limitations

The normalized WFP table spans 2002-01-15 to 2026-11-15 across all price flags, but pure actual market records end on 2026-04-15. The trained WFP test series are historical and most end in 2017; Maize (white) at Dawanau has later isolated observations that cannot supply a continuous lag window. NBS workbooks cover 2024-11 through 2026-05, but stable post-rebase item/geography series have only 17 months. The two sources are not combined or used as substitutes.

ABOUT THE PROJECT

Evidence for better decisions.

AgriPrice-NG is a research dashboard for understanding agricultural commodity prices in the Nigerian market.

AG
FINAL-YEAR COMPUTER SCIENCE PROJECT

Machine Learning Based Analysis and Prediction of Agricultural Commodities Prices in Nigerian Market.

The platform analyzes historical WFP Nigeria Food Prices data, compares forecasting approaches, and presents 1-, 3-, and 6-month estimates for the approved series retained by the research pipeline.

METHOD IN BRIEF

How the results are produced

  1. 01
    Prepare the series

    Monthly histories retain missing calendar months and preserve units, price types, and market identity.

  2. 02
    Engineer historical features

    Calendar-aware lags and rolling statistics are calculated without accessing future prices.

  3. 03
    Evaluate chronologically

    Models use chronological splits and common test observations where the audit permits comparison.

  4. 04
    Forecast recursively

    Supported forecasts feed predicted prices forward for the requested 1-, 3-, or 6-month horizon.

MODEL FAMILIES

What is compared

01Naive baseline

Last observed calendar-month price.

02Random Forest

Tree ensemble for engineered features.

03Gradient Boosting

Sequential boosted regression trees.

04XGBoost

Regularized gradient boosting.

05Hybrid stacking

Leakage-safe base models and meta-model.

06LSTM

Exploratory sequence model for aggregate series.

Scope note

WFP observations and World Bank close estimates remain separate sources. World Bank values are modelled/imputed estimates with source-provided low/high ranges and trust scores; they are not live survey prices. Missing periods are never silently filled.

AgriPrice-NG ? WFP + NBS Current-Data Series Selection Audit
=============================================================
Audit date: 2026-11-19
Scope: seven research commodity groups; market-level WFP candidate series and separate NBS item/geography mapping.
No models were trained. No target values were interpolated, forward-filled, synthesized, or combined across source/geography/unit.

Inputs and method
-----------------
WFP normalized input: data/processed/wfp_monthly.csv (91,216 rows; SHA-256 195b909893761807d1918a7674a9b05fc119b7bd4743c440da29d3a4a2318aab).
NBS normalized input: data/processed/nbs_monthly.csv (1,470 rows; SHA-256 e534f446b1d81a7e16e3ab9314d0281c73dfe9b9c519b711ec1a8b32e17c5422).
WFP candidate key: research mapping + exact source commodity + market + market_id + state + unit + price type + currency; only exact priceflag="actual" rows with non-missing prices are candidates.
WFP is already monthly-normalized by the existing ingestion process; source observation counts and exact within-month date bounds are retained. No calendar reindexing or price filling was performed.
Other WFP flags (aggregate and actual,aggregate) remain excluded and are not merged into pure actual series.
Trailing windows are anchored to the audit month: last 24 months = 2024-12 through 2026-11; last 36 months = 2023-12 through 2026-11.
Calendar coverage is observed actual months divided by all calendar months from the first through latest observation. A one-month series can show 100% span coverage; it is not evidence of a usable forecast history.

WFP freshness finding
---------------------
All WFP normalized rows extend through 2026-11-15 (2026-11), but this tail is not pure actual market data.
Pure actual WFP rows end at 2026-04-15 (2026-04); this latest actual is 218 days before the audit date.
Aggregate-flag rows end at 2026-11-15. Price-flag source-record counts are: {'actual': 51892, 'actual,aggregate': 1217, 'aggregate': 38107}.
Therefore the apparent November 2026 data tail must not be described as observed market prices: the pure actual source stops on 2026-04-15, and neither mixed actual,aggregate nor aggregate rows were used as direct market observations.

WFP series availability results
-------------------------------
Pure-actual market-level series in the scoped labels: 544 total; 374 direct/variant commodity matches and 170 related-but-not-equivalent products/pulses.
Of the direct/variant series, 53 have any 2026 actual observation. Every such latest observation is in April 2026.
Across all 544 scoped groups, 475 have zero observations and 69 have exactly one in the trailing 24 months; none has two or more.
No scoped series has more than one actual monthly observation in the trailing 36 months either. A 2026 point represents at most 1/24 months (4.17%) and 1/36 months (2.78%) in those windows.

Current-condition recommendation
---------------------------------
No WFP market-level series is recommended for forecasting present market conditions from the files currently available. The decisive issue is not lifetime row count: for every scoped market/unit/price-type group, there are at most one pure-actual monthly points in each of the trailing 24 and 36 months, and the freshest pure actual is seven calendar months old at audit time.
Do not train on, or label as current actual observations, the 2026 aggregate-flag tail. The 69 groups across direct and related mapped labels that have a 2026 pure-actual point (53 direct/variant groups and 16 related-product/pulse groups) have only a single April point in the recent windows; they are a data-refresh/watchlist, not a forecast shortlist.
Freshness by primary research category: Beans has some April actuals; Rice (local) and Rice (imported), Yam, Tomatoes, and Palm oil have some April actuals; Maize has no pure-actual 2026 market series; raw Cassava has no exact WFP commodity label. The presence of a single April point does not support a multi-step forecast.
Keep source commodity variants, markets, states, units, and Retail/Wholesale types separate. Beans and cowpeas remain distinct; maize flour is not maize grain; gari is not raw cassava. Existing old Dawanau/CMB series may remain useful for historical analysis only, not as present-condition series.
Recommended next data action: obtain subsequent monthly WFP releases with pure actual market observations for the same exact commodity-market-state-unit-price-type keys. Re-audit before training; do not bridge the 2023?2026 gaps or treat the first resumed point as continuity.

NBS audit and mapping
---------------------
Existing NBS source tree contains 5 monthly workbooks and 5 paired PDFs for 2026-01, 2026-02, 2026-03, 2026-04, 2026-05; normalized observations are 1,470 rows.
NBS coverage is 43 original item labels, 7 geographies (national Nigeria plus six zones), and five months (2026-01 to 2026-05). Geography coverage varies by item: 41 labels have all seven geographies; "Smoked fish" has the six zones only, while "Smoked fish (Mackerel)" has Nigeria national only. No additional historical NBS release is present in the existing project source files: data/raw contains only these five workbook/PDF pairs, and backups contains no Excel or PDF files.
NBS missing prices: 0; duplicate item/geography/month keys: 0. The workbook has no standardized unit column; NGN is supported by the paired official PDFs.
Defensible direct mappings: Local Rice (Broken) and Rice Local, short-Grained -> Rice (local), kept as separate source products; Rice Long-Grained (Imported) -> Rice (imported); Maize (Corn) Grains (White) sold loose -> Maize; Beans Brown and Beans white -> Beans; Yam Tuber -> Yam; Tomatoes, fresh -> Tomatoes; Palm oil, 75cl -> Palm oil (75cl remains in the original label).
Related, not equivalent: Garri Yellow and Garri white are cassava-derived processed products, not raw cassava. No raw cassava item is present. The other 32 NBS labels are outside the seven-commodity scope. Every original label is retained in the mapping CSV.
NBS recommendation: retain national and each zone as separate descriptive monthly series. Five months are insufficient for a defensible time-series forecast; do not merge NBS national/zone averages with WFP market-level data or infer standardized units.

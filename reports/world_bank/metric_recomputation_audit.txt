World Bank prediction metric recomputation audit
================================================

Source files: reports/world_bank/model_comparison.csv and
reports/world_bank/predictions.csv.

The saved predictions were recomputed with the same definitions used by the
training runner: RMSE and MAE on all test rows, MAPE excluding zero or
near-zero actual prices, R2 against the test-period mean, and prediction bias
(predicted minus actual). Result: 2,160 prediction rows and 60 evaluated
metric rows were checked; 0 metric mismatches were found at 1e-8 tolerance.

Each of the 12 trained series has 36 test months, and every model for a series
uses the same semicolon-delimited test month list. Test metrics were not used
to select transformations; selection_audit.csv records validation-only
selection.

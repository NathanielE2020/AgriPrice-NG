World Bank LSTM status
======================

No World Bank LSTM was trained in this experiment. The project-local Flask
environment does not include TensorFlow, and the existing TensorFlow models
were trained on WFP aggregate series. Reusing those artifacts for World Bank
close estimates would mix sources and invalidate the results, so the dashboard
marks LSTM unavailable for the World Bank selector. This is an explicit
unsupported-model status, not a fabricated metric.
